Prompt Engineering Assignment #4
Question 1: Zero-Shot Prompting
Task: Write a simple instruction asking the AI to summarize a story or decide if a review is positive or negative, without giving any examples.
Prompt:
Read the following customer review and determine whether it is Positive or Negative. Just reply with one word: Positive or Negative.  Review: "I ordered this laptop last week and it arrived broken. The screen has cracks and the keyboard doesn't work. Customer support was unhelpful and rude. I will never buy from this store again."

Question 3: Few-Shot Prompting
Task: Give the AI three examples of a specific writing style, then ask it to write a new one in the same style.
Here are three short product descriptions written in a fun, energetic, and casual style:  1. "This water bottle is your new gym BFF — keeps drinks icy cold for 24 hours and looks amazing doing it!"  2. "Say goodbye to boring snacks! Our granola bars are packed with flavor and the energy to crush your day."  3. "Meet your new morning hero — a coffee mug that keeps your brew hot while you pretend to be a 
morning person."  Now write a new product description in the same fun and energetic style for: a wireless phone charger.
Question 4: System Prompting
Task: Write a set of main rules for an AI — how it must behave, what it cannot say, and what tone to use with customers.
You are a professional customer support assistant for ShopEase, an online retail store. Follow these rules at all times:  1. Always greet the customer warmly and use their name if provided. 2. Be polite, patient, and empathetic — even if the customer is frustrated. 3. Never argue with the customer or use rude/dismissive language. 4. Do not discuss competitor brands or make negative comments about other companies. 5. Only provide information related to orders, products, returns, and shipping. 6. If you cannot solve the issue, politely escalate to a human agent. 7. Always end the conversation by asking if there is anything else you can help with.
Question 5: Role Prompting
Task: Write a prompt that tells the AI to act as a professional expert (doctor, lawyer, or historian) and ask it a domain-specific question.
You are an experienced history professor specializing in World War II and 20th-century European history. Answer the following question the way a professional historian would — with context, key causes, and significant consequences.  Question: What were the primary causes of World War II, and how did the Treaty of Versailles contribute to its outbreak?
Question 6: Contextual Prompting
Task: Provide the AI with a lot of background information first, then ask it to create a plan based only on that information.
Here is some background information about my business:  - Business Name: GreenLeaf Cafe - Type: Small organic coffee and smoothie cafe - Location: Downtown area, high foot traffic - Target Customers: Health-conscious young professionals aged 25–40 - Current Problem: Sales have dropped 30% in the last 3 months - Budget for marketing: $500/month - Social media presence: Instagram (800 followers), no other platforms - Competitors: Two large coffee chains nearby offering discounts - Strengths: Unique organic menu, cozy atmosphere, loyal regular customers  Based only on this information, create a 3-step marketing plan to help GreenLeaf Cafe recover its sales.
